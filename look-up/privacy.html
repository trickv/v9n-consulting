Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

Look Up ("the App") is operated by Patrick van Staveren ("I", "me"). This policy describes how the App handles your information.

Information the App Uses On-Device

The App accesses the following device capabilities to function. This data is processed entirely on your device and is not transmitted to any server:

  • Location — Your GPS coordinates are used to calculate the positions of celestial objects in the sky above you. Your location is never sent off your device.
  • Device Orientation — Compass heading and accelerometer data are used to align the sky chart and augmented reality view with the direction you are facing.
  • Camera — The camera is used solely for the augmented reality view, overlaying celestial object information on a live preview of the sky. No images or video are captured, stored, or transmitted.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App uses Sentry for crash reporting and performance analytics. Sentry may collect:

  • Crash logs and error diagnostics
  • Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • General usage analytics and performance data

This data is used solely to identify and fix bugs and improve app stability. It does not include your location, camera imagery, or any personal content. Sentry's privacy policy is available at sentry.io/privacy.

Data Sharing

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties. The only data that leaves your device is the crash and analytics information sent to Sentry as described above.

Data Retention

The App does not store personal data on any server I operate. Crash and analytics data retained by Sentry is subject to Sentry's data retention policies.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact me so I can delete it.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
